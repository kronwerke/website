Privacy

This notice covers the Kronwerke website, the Minecraft server and the Discord bot. It follows the Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection (FADP) and, where it applies, the GDPR. The site uses no tracking and embeds nothing from other providers; a cookie is set only when you sign in to the whitelist app.

2. Website

With every request a browser sends its IP address, the time, the address requested, the browser and the operating system. The server needs this to deliver the page and to protect it from abuse (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). It keeps no access log for this site; the data is only processed while the connection lasts.

Once a minute the page asks our server whether the Minecraft server is running and how far the current community goal is. The same applies, nothing is stored.

One thing your browser keeps by itself, without cookies and without us seeing it: which steps of the install guide you have ticked. Clearing the site data of your browser removes it.

If you sign in to the whitelist app in a browser, the page sets one technically necessary cookie for at most ten minutes, which secures the sign in with Discord. After that your browser keeps the sign in for that tab only, until you close it. What the bot handles there is on the Discord bot page.

The start page also asks Discord once how many people are online on our Discord (the widget API of Discord Inc.). Discord sees your IP address for that, as with any visit to Discord itself; Discord's privacy policy applies.

3. Minecraft server

When you play, the server processes your Minecraft name, your account ID, your IP address and what you do in the game: chat, world data, progress, contributions to the community goals and how long you play. This is needed so you can play (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR). Name and IP address are in the server logs, which are deleted after 30 days. World data is kept for the season.

Action log (Chronik). So the team can trace and undo griefing (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR), the server records who placed, broke or changed which block, what was taken out of or put into chests and machines, what someone killed, dropped or picked up, the chat, commands typed, and joins and leaves, each with place and time. For private messages (/msg, /tell, /w and the like) only the receiver is kept, never the text. The log is deleted after 30 days. Only team members with operator rights can read it.

Play times. The server's admin console shows the team when you were on which server. This list is deleted after 30 days. Team members who may only watch see no IP addresses in the console and the logs.

Voice chat. Voice chat runs through the server and is not stored. Recording is turned off on the server, for the players too.

What is public. The website and the status page show only how many people are playing, no names. The status message on the Discord names the players; only verified members of the Discord see it. Contributions to the obelisk show with your Minecraft name in the game, on the status page and in the streamers' stream overlay, since filling the goals together is the heart of the game.

4. Discord and the bot

On the Kronwerke Discord, the privacy policy of Discord Inc. applies. What our bot processes there is described in detail on the Discord bot page.

4a. Twitch bot

In the Twitch channels of the streamers taking part, a bot answers commands like !start. For that it reads the chat of those channels. It keeps the latest messages in memory only, so the team sees them in its overview; they are not stored and are gone with a restart. What a viewer writes after a command, the bot repeats only without links and shortened.

5. Hosting and sharing

The website and the bot run on a server of 24fire GmbH in Germany, the Minecraft server at Tavuru (tavuru.com). Both process the data only on our behalf. Beyond that we share no data, sell none and use none for advertising.

6. Your rights

You can ask what data we hold about you, and have it corrected, deleted or restricted. You can object to the processing and get your data in a common format. Write to legal@elchi.dev; we answer within 30 days.

7. Security

Connections to the website and the bot are encrypted (TLS). Only the Kronwerke team has access to the servers and the data.

8. Changes

If anything changes, it appears here with a new date.

9. Supervisory authorities

In Switzerland this is the Federal Data Protection and Information Commissioner (FDPIC), in the EU the data protection authority of your country.
