Questions

What most people want to know before they start. Your question is missing? Ask on the Discord.

Playing

For everyone who wants on the server.

What is Kronwerke?

A Minecraft server with a big tech and magic modpack, where streamers play with their communities. The server only moves on together: new stages open when everyone together fills a goal at the obelisk. More on the stages page (German).

When does it start?

Season 2 starts on Saturday, 16 January 2027, at 6 pm German time (CET). In December there is an event with the streamers before that. A season runs six to eight weeks.

Can I play already?

No, the server opens on 16 January at 6 pm German time. What you can do now: join the Discord, get a place and install the pack, then you are in on the first day.

How do I get a place?

Join the Discord and solve the captcha (if you cannot read the image, there is a button that asks the team to let you in). Every streamer has places for their community and puts you on the list. If you played in Season 1, /link with your Minecraft name is enough: you are on right away and have two places of your own for friends.

Do I have to stay on the Discord?

Yes. Whoever leaves the Discord loses their place on the whitelist, and the places they gave become free.

What is an origin?

On your first join you pick an origin and a role: an origin shapes how you play, with advantages and one real drawback, a role gives three small bonuses without a drawback. All nine origins and seven roles are on the origins page (German).

I join later, do I stand a chance?

Yes. Whoever joins later gets the starter kits of every open stage and catches up quickly. The goals are filled by the whole server, there is nothing you have to make up for.

Is there land protection?

Yes. With FTB Chunks you claim your chunks on the map; others cannot break or open anything there, fire and lava from outside do not get in, and there is no PvP in claims unless your team turns it on. Teams share their claims with friends.

Is there voice chat?

Yes, Simple Voice Chat is in the pack. On the first start it asks for the microphone; you set the push to talk key in Voice Chat's menu. Recording is turned off on the server.

Is everything in English?

Menus, hints, items and the Kronwerke screens are. The quest book is German for now; an English one follows. Most of the community speaks German, English is welcome in chat.

Does it cost anything?

No. Kronwerke is a community project by Elchi Studios and free.

Technical

Installing, memory, crashes.

Which version do I need?

Minecraft Java 1.21.1 with NeoForge, the pack brings both. How to install it is under Install.

How much memory do I need?

Give the game 8 GB, at least 6 GB. Your PC should have 16 GB. With the 2 GB many launchers preset, the pack does not start properly. The memory helper tells you what fits your PC.

Can I add my own mods?

Mods that only change graphics or controls are usually fine. Anything that gives you an advantage, like seeing through walls, is forbidden. If in doubt, ask on the Discord first.

Loading gets stuck or the game crashes

Check the memory first (see above). If that does not help, write in the support channel on the Discord and attach the file logs/latest.log from your instance.

An item only shows three question marks

Then it belongs to a stage that is not open yet. You can pick it up and store it, but not use it. Once the stage opens, it gets its name. JEI already shows the items of the next stage, so you can plan.

The sky on the website stutters

The sky measures what your device manages and switches to a still image when it gets tight. You can also pause it yourself at the bottom of the home page.

Streamers

Applying, places, events, overlay.

I am a streamer, how do I join?

Apply on the Discord with /apply. The team gets back to you in a private channel. What you get and what we expect is on the streamer page (German).

How many places do I get for my community?

Every streamer gets a base number of places the team sets. You give them in the whitelist app: on Discord with /whitelist verwalten or in the browser at kronwerke.com/whitelist, with skin faces and search. /whitelist add still works. If you need more places, ask right in the app.

Do I have to be at the events?

Yes, that is the heart of it. When a goal is almost full, the obelisk stops taking items, there is a date, and all streamers go live together when the last items go in. Five such dates per season.

Is there an overlay for my stream?

Yes. The current community goal with its pillars, the milestones and every deposit live, as a browser source for OBS and Streamlabs, with a transparent background. Addresses and settings are on the overlay page.

Nothing here on that. Ask on the Discord and it gets added.
